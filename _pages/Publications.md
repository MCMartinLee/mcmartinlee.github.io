## Publications
My research focus on multimodal conversational AI including turn managament, eye gaze mimic, and gesture generation:

{% include feature_row id="bar" type="left" %}

[**[arXiv / Microsoft Research] RESPOND: Responsive Engagement Strategy for Predictive Orchestration and Dialogue**](/projects/RESPOND)

A real-time conversational AI framework that moves beyond turn-based interaction by enabling agents to predict *when* and *how* to respond during ongoing speech. Introduces controllable interaction strategies, including backchannel intensity and turn-claim aggressiveness, allowing agents to engage in more natural, continuous, and human-like dialogue.

---

[**[Sci. Data] Multi-TPC: A Multimodal Dataset for Three-Party Conversations with Speech, Motion, and Gaze. Sci Data (2026).**](/projects/MultiTPC)

A large-scale multimodal dataset of three-party conversations with synchronized speech, motion, and gaze, designed to support research on turn-taking, attention, and interaction dynamics in realistic multi-party settings.

---

[**[ICMI'25] Enhancing Gaze Prediction in Multi-Party Conversations via Speaker-Aware Multimodal Adaptation**](https://graphics.cs.uh.edu/wp-content/papers/2025/2025-ICMI-GazePredictionFromText.pdf)

Developed SAT5, a speaker-aware adaptation of the T5 model, to predict gaze directions in three-party conversations using only linguistic and speaker cues, without requiring direct visual input, enabling scalable gaze modeling from text.

---

[**[ICMI'25] Learning Multimodal Motion Cues for Online End-of-Turn Prediction in Multi-Party Dialogue**](https://graphics.cs.uh.edu/wp-content/papers/2025/2025-ICMI-LearningMultimodalCuesforEOT.pdf)

Proposed a real-time end-of-turn prediction framework that leverages symbolic motion representations learned via VQ-VAE (head, hand, and full-body motion), achieving improved performance over traditional multimodal baselines in multi-party dialogue.

---

[**[ICMI'24] Online Multimodal End-of-Turn Prediction for Three-party Conversations**](https://graphics.cs.uh.edu/wp-content/papers/2024/2024-ICMI-OnlineTurnPrediction.pdf)  
<i class="fas fa-award" style="color:orange"></i> <span style="color:orange">Best Paper Runner Up</span>

Introduced a multimodal PLM-GRU framework for online end-of-turn prediction in three-party conversations, integrating text, prosody, and gaze features to model turn-taking behavior in real time.

---

[**[IVA'24] Computational Study on Sentence-based Next Speaker Prediction in Multiparty Conversations**](https://graphics.cs.uh.edu/wp-content/papers/2024/2024-IVA-SetenceLevelNextSpeakerStudy.pdf)

Conducted a systematic study on sentence-level next-speaker prediction, comparing different modeling strategies and demonstrating how linguistic structure can be leveraged to improve speaker transition prediction in multi-party dialogue.

---

[**[ICMI'23] Multimodal Turn Prediction and Analysis for Multiparty Conversations**](https://graphics.cs.uh.edu/wp-content/uploads/papers/2023/2023-ICMI-MultimodalTurnAnalysis.pdf)

Presented an early multimodal framework for analyzing and predicting turn-taking behavior in multi-party conversations, combining speech, gaze, and interaction signals to better understand conversational dynamics.
